JustWaitAI

JustWaitAI is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the JustWait AI YouTube channel, to plan and prepare videos for that channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

With the owner's permission, it:

  • reads the channel's own YouTube statistics (read-only access);
  • if upload permission is granted, uploads a video to the owner's own channel only after the owner approves that specific video.

It is not given permission to delete or edit existing videos, comments, or account settings.
